# AGY source search: controlled LPO trade-offs

Use Gemini 3.7 Flash web search. Source discovery only.

Comparison target:

- target: 800G DR8 LPO SiPh front-panel pluggable;
- baseline: 800G DR8 retimed front-panel pluggable;
- preferred controls: same aggregate rate, lane count/rate, reach, media, FEC/BER boundary, temperature, form factor, connector and measurement boundary.

Find public primary evidence for conditional advantages, costs, new bottlenecks and alternatives. Search especially for:

- module power and latency boundaries;
- removal/relocation of DSP/CDR/retiming responsibilities;
- host SerDes/channel-loss/FEC dependence;
- interoperability, link training, testing, calibration, yield/reliability or operational constraints;
- where a comparison is not controlled.

Use official standards/MSA/OIF papers, peer-reviewed papers, and first-party product measurements. Company marketing claims must be labeled `company-stated`, not fact. Prefer direct tables/figures with test conditions.

For each atomic claim return exact query, URL, document metadata, page/section, short quote, conditions, evidence level, comparator, and explicit limitations. If no controlled evidence exists, say `NOT_COMPARABLE` and list missing controls.

Do not produce an unconditional ranking, company winner list, market conclusion, WHY link, QID mapping, or knowledge-base write recommendation.
