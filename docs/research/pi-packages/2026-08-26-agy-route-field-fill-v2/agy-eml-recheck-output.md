### Contradiction Ledger

1. **Heatsink Code Definition (`A`) vs. Mechanical Suffix (`-2N`) and Drawing:**
   * *Datasheet Product Selection (Page 1):* Defines character `A` as `"Dual MPO-12 receptacle, Closed Heatsink"`, while defining suffix `2N` as `"No Heat Sink Design"`.
   * *Datasheet Section XI & Figure 2 (Page 6):* States `"compatible with the OSFP Specification for pluggable form factor type 2 modules"` and depicts a flat-top module without integrated heatsink fins.
   * *Impact:* Code `A` nominally specifies an integrated closed heatsink, but the `-2N` suffix overrides the physical design to a flat-top (riding heatsink) OSFP Type 2 form factor.

2. **Operating Temperature Scope in Environmental Specifications:**
   * *Page 1 (Product Features & Selection) & Page 2 (Section II):* Expressly defines two temperature options: Commercial ($0^\circ\text{C}$ to $+70^\circ\text{C}$, option `C`) and Limited ($+20^\circ\text{C}$ to $+60^\circ\text{C}$, option `L`).
   * *Page 5 (Section VI Environmental Specifications):* Text and table list only $0^\circ\text{C}$ to $+70^\circ\text{C}$ (`"Finisar FTCE4527E1PxA-2N DR8+ OSFP transceivers have an operating case temperature range of 0°C to +70°C."`), omitting the $+20^\circ\text{C}$ to $+60^\circ\text{C}$ range defined on Pages 1 and 2.

3. **Section Numbering Inconsistencies:**
   * *Page 5:* Digital Diagnostics is mislabeled as `"III. Digital Diagnostics Functions"`, colliding with `"III. Electrical Characteristics"` on Page 3.
   * *Page 6:* Mechanical Specifications jumps to `"XI. Mechanical Specifications"` directly after Section IX on Page 5, skipping Section X.

4. **Optical PMD & Application Standard Cross-Referencing:**
   * *Document Title & Scope (Pages 1, 5, 6):* Designated as `"800G-DR8+ OSFP Optical Finisar® Transceiver FTCE4527E1PxA-2N"` supporting up to 2 km SMF.
   * *Optical Characteristics Header (Page 3, Section IV):* States `"Meets 4x100GBASE-FR1 as defined by IEEE P802.3cu"`.
   * *General Specifications (Page 5, Section V, Note 1):* States `"Supports 400GBASE-DR4 per IEEE P802.3bs"`.
   * *Applications (Page 1):* Simultaneously lists `"800G DR8+ applications with FEC"` and `"2 X 400 DR4+ applications with FEC"`.

### Unresolved Gaps

1. **Laser Architecture / Transmitter Type:** The first-party datasheet does not explicitly state whether the 1310 nm optical source is an Electro-absorption Modulated Laser (EML), a continuous-wave laser paired with Silicon Photonics (CW+SiPh), or a directly modulated DFB.
2. **Internal Thermoelectric Cooler (TEC):** The datasheet contains no evidence, specifications, or mention of an internal thermoelectric cooler (the parameters `TDECQ` and `TECQ` in Section IV refer strictly to IEEE PAM4 Transmitter Eye Closure metrics, not active thermal stabilization hardware).
3. **Internal Component Vendors & Process Technology:** No disclosure is provided regarding DSP semiconductor fabrication node (e.g., 7nm / 5nm / 4nm), DSP supplier, analog driver/TIA circuitry, optical subassembly architecture (TOSA/ROSA vs. discrete vs. PIC), or optical lens coupling mechanics.
4. **Commercial Availability / Active Ordering Status:** Document is titled `Preliminary Product Specification` (Rev A1, Oct. 2023). Regulatory compliance certificates (FDA, UL) confirm safety certifications but do not establish general commercial availability (GA), active ordering availability, or volume mass production.
5. **Exact Orderable SKU Table:** The datasheet provides the configurable syntax `FTCE4527E1PxA-2N` and shows `FTCE4527E1PCA-2N` on the sample label, but does not provide a discrete commercial orderable SKU lookup catalog.
